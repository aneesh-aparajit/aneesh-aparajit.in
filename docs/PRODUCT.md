## Platform

## Stack

Next.js (App Router). Content pages should be statically generated, since the site is mostly content. The Markdown/MDX content pipeline for posts and projects is undecided. Deploy target is Vercel's free Hobby plan, which is for personal, non-commercial use and fits a portfolio.

## Users

- **Recruiters and hiring managers** evaluating the owner for a software engineering role. They arrive from a resume, LinkedIn, or a referral, skim quickly, and need to answer "what has this person built, and should we talk?" — then reach out or grab the resume.
- **Engineers and peers** who arrive via a blog post, a GitHub repo, or a shared link. They read more deeply, judge technical substance, and may follow the work or the writing.

A personal portfolio and writing home for Aneesh Aparajit (name taken from the GitHub account `aneesh-aparajit`; confirm display name). It presents projects, writing, and work history so that both audiences can quickly verify real engineering ability. Success: a recruiter can find the resume and a way to make contact within seconds; an engineer finds something worth reading and comes back.

The site's claim is demonstrated, inspectable work — real repositories and technical writing — rather than self-description.

## Operating Context

- Recruiters scan; engineers read. Both need fast paths to projects, writing, and resume/contact.

## Brand Commitments

- **Minimalist, but designed.** The owner is a minimalist: nothing fancy or showy, but not a bare text page either. Some deliberate design is welcome.
- **Clarity over flourish.** A visitor should quickly understand what the owner brings, what they can build, and where to read more.
- **Writing matters.** The owner is starting to write engineering blog posts and wants to be more active in the engineering community, so the blog is a growing, primary part of the site.

## Product Principles

1. **Show the work.** Every claim about ability should point at something inspectable — a repo, a post, a role.
2. **Two speeds.** Serve the 30-second recruiter skim and the 10-minute engineer read on the same site without making either wait for the other.
3. **Writing is first-class.** Posts are a primary reason to visit, not an appendix; reading them should be comfortable.
4. **Fast and durable.** Static, light, and easy to keep updated by adding Markdown — maintenance cost must stay near zero.
